# Changelog

## v1.0.1 — 22 Nov 2026
- Steadier cursor: small hand shakes are ignored and the cursor glides between camera frames
- Clicks land where you aim: the cursor holds still for a moment as a pinch starts or ends
- No ghost cursors: one-hand screens track one hand, and weak or duplicate detections are ignored
- A new hand needs a few frames before it gets a cursor

## v1.0.0 — 21 Nov 2026
- First release: Draw in the Air, Colour & Paint, Trace & Learn, Air Games, Air Music, Sticker World, Gallery, Grown-ups area
- Works offline (installable web app) plus a Windows installer and portable .exe
